How to Approach the Case

Disco Elysium is a role-playing detective game. You explore, talk with people, inspect clues, and decide how Harry responds. There is no single required way to play.

Skills and Checks

Harry's skills influence what he notices and how conversations unfold. Some actions use dice checks, so outcomes can depend on both your character and chance. A failed check is not always a dead end; it can lead to a different moment in the story.

Thought Cabinet

Ideas encountered during the investigation can be considered in the Thought Cabinet. Thinking about an idea takes time in the game and can affect Harry's skills or conversations.

Beginner Tips

  • Talk to people and revisit conversations when you learn more.
  • Examine the environment for objects and details you can investigate.
  • Save your game before trying a risky action.
  • Let the story unfold naturally; different choices can create different experiences.
