This page collects self-service links and explains how to contact the student website author. The linked online resources can be visited at any time. No staffed support hours are listed here.

Support Categories

  1. Gameplay Help

    For general game information or help from other players, visit the game's official page and its community guides.

    • Disco Elysium on Steam - game information and community resources.
    • Steam Community Guides - player-created guides.
    • Availability: online self-service; no staffed hours are listed here.

  2. Technical Troubleshooting

    If you need help with the game, use the platform's online support resources. This website is an independent student guide and cannot troubleshoot the game or access player accounts.

    • Steam Help for Disco Elysium - online help for the Steam version.
    • Availability: online self-service; support hours are not specified on this page.

  3. Website Feedback

    Share a correction or comment about this student website using the contact form. The form is not connected to a submission service, so it cannot currently send or save messages.

    • Open the website contact form.
    • Availability: this page and form are online at any time.
    • Staffed support: no staffed hours are offered or listed for this website.
